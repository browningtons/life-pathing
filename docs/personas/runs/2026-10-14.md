## What worked (keep it)
- The arithmetic is right and shown. Dave checked six dates by hand, including 11, 22 and 33, and the temperament and adjacent-type formulas. Every number matched.
- The letters ask is fast on a phone: one-thumb auto-advance, Back keeps answers, "-A/-T" and lowercase accepted. Every persona reached a type without leaving.
- The paste parser is forgiving (lowercase, missing %, "81 percent", a stray "Mood:" prefix, 150% rejected) and the Profile visibly moves with the pasted numbers.
- The sample banner ("The numbers here belong to the person who built this") earned trust from eight of ten.
- "Where the Systems Agree" and "The Inner Cast" were the sections three personas called "a letter about me".
- No account, no email, and no second Buy control in the paid state.

## Lessons
1. The panel found the product gap, not the bugs, in the first five minutes: all ten reports lead with W1. A re-run after phase 2 is the measurement that matters.
2. Text-matched clicks are dangerous when a destructive button's label contains a tab's name. Say "click by ref" in the brief, and read a persona's "no confirm" as the harness first.
3. The harness cannot drive a `prompt()`-based flow. An email restore needs either a config field for the prompt answer or a form in the app.
4. Analytics that are not switched on are the first finding of any panel. Check before casting, and put the answer in the report.
