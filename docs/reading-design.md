# Design: The Crossover Reading

## Problem Statement

Life Pathing is a free reader's tool for two old self-reflection systems: numerology by birthdate and MBTI by type. The goal is real income: a free tier that gets found and a paid tier a stranger buys. The April 2026 paywall gated content every free site gives away (strengths, shadows, famous names) and was pulled in a day. A free/paid split shipped on 2026-09-10 that gates the deeper Profile sections, but those sections need 23 facet scores from a paid Truity report, which the buyer this design targets does not have. The paid tier needs a headline product that is personal, kept, and only available here.

## Demand Evidence

None for this app. The evidence is a market claim: similar apps are selling, naming astrology apps (Co-Star, The Pattern, Sanctuary) and numerology report sites (Numerologist.com and similar). Vercel Web Analytics is not enabled on the deployed project, so there is no traffic or funnel data. The Reading is therefore built as the instrument that measures demand, not as a response to demand already seen.

Landscape (generic web search, 2026-09-12): numerology reports sell one-time at $17 to $57 (Numerologist.com), $35 for a 20-reading bundle (World Numerology), about $56 hand-written on Etsy. Astrology apps run subscriptions at $8 to $22 a month. The Pattern's personality-based readings hold sessions two to four times longer than plain horoscopes. Paywalls fail when they fire before the user sees a personal result, when onboarding shows features instead of outcomes, and when positioning is broad. Nobody in the category combines MBTI type with numerology.

## Status Quo

The birthdate-only reader gets life-path meanings free from a hundred sites and Co-Star free. When they want something kept, they buy a $17 to $57 PDF from a report site or an Etsy seller who emails it within 48 hours. Their workaround costs money and gives them a generic number report. Nothing they can buy today crosses their number with their type.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge

**The buyer:** the birthdate-only reader who arrives from a search ("life path 7 meaning") or a shared link, late at night, with a date and a name. Not the Truity report owner. Not yet the couple.

**The wedge:** the Crossover Reading. One document, 700 to 900 words, written for this life path (with its compound number) and this four-letter type, in the app's quiet-almanac voice. Generated once per unique combination, cached, shown on the page, printable to PDF. The opening paragraph is free so the paywall fires after a personal result, not before. One-time, $19.

**The inputs, v1:** birthdate (already collected; reduces to life path and compound) plus four letters, which come from a twelve-question in-app forced-choice quiz or are typed if the reader knows them. Nothing else. Facet scores, birthday number, personal year, and name numbers are out of v1 (see Scope decisions).

## Constraints

- No accounts, no database of users. Device entitlement plus email restore, as today. The server store holds three things and nothing personal: Readings keyed on life path, compound label, and type; a set of hashed generation keys per hashed Stripe session id (for the cap); and a daily generation counter. No birthdate, no email, no raw session id.
- One-time payment via the existing Stripe Payment Link and verify-purchase route. One SKU.
- The almanac voice is the product. Generated text is constrained by a fixed outline, named voice exemplars from the app's own copy, and a banned-word list.
- Nothing medical, psychological, or life advice. The existing disclaimer framing holds on every page and in the system prompt. No section may forecast.
- Vercel serverless only, on the team's Pro plan; the generation route sets an explicit `maxDuration`.

## Premises

1. The buyer is the birthdate-only reader from search or a share. They do not have facet scores and never will. **Revised after second opinion:** the four letters come from a short in-app quiz, or are typed if known. The reader is never sent to another site's test.
2. Their status quo is free life-path pages and Co-Star's free tier; when they want something kept they buy a $17 to $57 PDF. The paid thing must be personal and kept, not more reference content. Free content is the funnel, not the product.
3. The Reading is generated from life path + compound + four letters, in the almanac voice with a fixed outline, cached by that key. Hand-writing the pairings is a later moat, not the first version.
4. One-time price of $19. One SKU. No accounts.
5. The facet Profile gate shipped on 2026-09-10 stays and is unlocked by the same SKU. The Reading is the headline and the upgrade modal sells it.
6. Done means: a stranger who arrived with only a birthdate buys a Reading.

## Cross-Model Perspective

Independent cold read by a fresh Claude subagent (Codex not installed), given a structured summary only.

- **Steelman:** a one-time, kept artifact nobody else sells, laying a 16-type portrait over a 12-number life path in a restrained voice; both inputs free to obtain; the free Life Path and Archetype pages are an SEO funnel app-store astrology apps cannot run; the later moat is hand-edited pairings plus a pair-read upsell.
- **Most revealing answer:** that the demand evidence is other apps selling. The demand case is about astrology subscriptions and numerology PDF mills, not this product. The Analytics component fires into a disabled Vercel setting. The most valuable build is the funnel that lets the Reading be judged; build the Reading as the instrument for measuring demand.
- **Challenged premise:** premise 1. A search visitor will not leave to take a 60-question test and come back; that handoff is where the funnel dies. Evidence: after analytics go live, a drop above 80 percent between "Life Path shown" and "letters entered." Fix: a twelve-question in-app quiz producing letters, not facets. **Accepted; premise 1 revised.**
- **48-hour prototype:** api/reading.ts beside verify-purchase.ts; re-verify the session; cache by input hash; one Claude call with the life-path entry, the type entry, the compound label, a fixed outline, app copy as voice exemplars, a hard ban on "journey", "energy", "universe"; 700 to 900 words; ReadingView gated by useGate; window.print with a print stylesheet instead of jsPDF; upgrade copy leads with the Reading; funnel events; ship at $19; skip prerender, share card, pair read, email delivery, Buy Button.

The cold read's cache key (life path + letters) is adopted, with the compound number added. An earlier draft of this design proposed keying on the full birthdate to carry birthday number and personal year; adversarial review showed that adds unwritten numerology code, a time-varying key, a forecasting section that strains the no-advice constraint, and a cache of birthdates. All of that moved to v2.

## Scope decisions (from adversarial review)

- **v1 inputs are life path + compound + four letters.** No facets: the buyer has none, and the profile normalizer backfills missing facets from the bundled sample, so a "facets when present" rule would have written the founder's facets into every quiz-only buyer's Reading. The Reading never reads facets. (The Profile tab's facet sections showing sample facets to a quiz-only reader is a separate product bug; see Dependencies.)
- **No personal year, no birthday number, no name numbers in v1.** Six sections, not seven. These come back in v2 once twenty Readings have been read for voice, and then the cache key grows to carry them.
- **Teaser is a separate short call.** Not the first paragraph of the full generation. See Generation contract.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: Generated Reading, cached (chosen)
One Claude call per unique key, fixed outline, voice exemplars, banned words, cached. ReadingView renders it; window.print for PDF; opening paragraph free via a separate short call. Twelve-question quiz. Analytics on. $19. Effort M, risk medium. Reuses verify-purchase.ts, LIFE_PATH_MEANINGS, MBTI_DATA, COMPOUND_MEANINGS, getSynthesis, the convergence and growth blocks, the kit gate.

### Approach B: Hand-written blocks, generated joins
Founder writes 12 life-path portraits, 16 type portraits, and 48 temperament-by-life-path convergence and tension blocks; the model writes only the joining sections and the closing line. Strongest voice and a real content moat, at roughly 76 blocks of writing before the first sale. Effort L, risk low. This is A plus writing, not instead of A; it remains the path once the Reading has buyers.

### Approach C: Wizard-of-Oz demand test
Quiz, free opening paragraph, real Stripe button, no generation route; first buyers get their Reading by email within 24 hours, produced by hand. Cheapest answer to "does anyone pay," but with no traffic a fake door and a real door see the same zero visitors, and it adds an email seam to the no-account model. Effort S, risk low.

## Recommended Approach

**A.** Four to six days of work in two phases, it produces both the product and the measuring instrument, and B's hand-written blocks can be swapped in underneath it later without changing what the buyer sees.

**Phase 1 (about two days): the funnel.** Analytics on, the five events, the letters ask on the Life Path tab, the quiz, the typed-letters path, the `dimensionSource` field, the Profile "no facet scores yet" state. Ship it, post the link (The Assignment), measure for seven days.

**Phase 2 (about three days): the Reading.** The Reading tab, the teaser call, the generation route with store, validator, regeneration, cap, ceiling, and kill switch, the print stylesheet, the $19 price and Payment Link, the privacy and refund page, the modal copy, the roadmap update.

### The Reading path, three screens

Wireframe: [`design/reading-sketch.html`](design/reading-sketch.html), screenshot at [`design/reading-sketch.png`](design/reading-sketch.png).

1. **Arrive with a birthdate.** The Life Path result is shown as today, on the Life Path tab. Directly under it, once the reader has entered a birthdate: "Your Reading. One document, written for a 7 who is also a … we need four letters for that." Two buttons: "I know my type" (four-letter input) and "Two-minute quiz." The ask sits after the first free result, never before it.

   **Provenance.** `isSample` clears the moment a birthdate is entered, while the dimensions are still the bundled sample's, so it cannot gate the ask. The profile gains a field, `dimensionSource: 'sample' | 'quiz' | 'typed' | 'report'`, default `'sample'`, set by the quiz, the typed path, and the paste-in intake respectively, and normalized like the rest of the profile. The ask shows while `dimensionSource === 'sample'`; the Reading tab requires it not to be. Every write path assigns it: the quiz → `'quiz'`; the typed four letters → `'typed'`; the paste-in intake and the Your Data per-field dimension controls → `'report'`; `resetProfile` → `'sample'`; the Archetypes click-to-toggle leaves it unchanged. `normalizeProfile` defaults a missing field to `'sample'` and `profilesEqual` compares it, so a profile whose only change is its source still differs from the bundled sample and persists.

   **The sample banner** changes its condition from `isSample` to `dimensionSource === 'sample'` and its copy to say what is still borrowed: "The type here is the sample's. Enter your own letters." On the Life Path tab its call to action scrolls to the letters ask; on other tabs it goes to the Your Data tab as today. The old `isSample` remains for the reset button and nothing else.

   **Typed path.** Four letters typed write 70/30 toward each chosen letter through `setDimensionScore`, outside the borderline band, and set the source to `'typed'`.
2. **Quiz.** Twelve forced-choice pairs, three per dimension, one at a time with a progress bar. Produces letters only, written into the profile's dimension percentages through `setDimensionScore` by a fixed table: 3 to 0 → 80/20, 2 to 1 → 55/45, and sets the source to `'quiz'`. A 2-to-1 split therefore lands inside the app's existing 45 to 55 borderline band and is flagged the way the app already flags it. A dead-even split is impossible with three questions.
3. **The Reading.** A new "Reading" tab. Type, life path, and temperament pills. The teaser paragraph, free. Then the line, rendered by the existing `ProGate` with three small extensions: `GateKey` gains `'reading'` with its own `GATE_COPY` entry ("Five more sections, written for this number and this type," naming them); `ProGate` takes an optional button label so this one reads "Read the whole thing" instead of "See the whole profile"; and its analytics source becomes `${surface}_${gate}` so this gate reports as `reading_reading` rather than under the `profile_` prefix. `ProGate` already fires `gate_shown` with the gate key. After purchase the full text renders on the same tab with Print / Save as PDF (a print stylesheet, which does not exist today; no jsPDF). On the post-checkout redirect (`#session_id`), when the unlock verifies, the shell switches to the Reading tab so the buyer lands on what they bought. The switch fires on the false-to-true transition of `justPurchased` only, never on its initial value, since that flag is sessionStorage-backed and starts true on every reload until dismissed. The post-purchase banner copy changes to name the Reading.

### Entitlement and SKU

One SKU, $19, product-level match in verify-purchase as today. It unlocks the Reading and the facet Profile sections together. In `kit.config.ts` four things change together: `upgrade.price`, `upgrade.features` (leading with the Reading), `stripe.paymentUrl` (the new $19 Payment Link, which is what the modal actually sends buyers to while `buyButtonId` is a placeholder), and `stripe.priceIdHint`. Changing the displayed price alone would ship a $19 modal that charges $29. The existing $29 price is retired in Stripe; buyers who already paid $29 verify on the product id and get everything.

The generation route requires a verified Stripe Checkout session id. The redirect path already stores it; the email-restore path must too: the POST branch of verify-purchase returns the matched session id and the client saves it alongside the Pro flag. The route caps generations at five unique keys per session (a buyer can change their letters a few times; nobody can farm it), stored as `sha256(sessionId) → set of sha256(key)`, so the store never holds a raw session id or an identifiable buyer's numerology.

**A Pro reader with no stored session id** (an email restore from before this ships, or lost storage) sees a "Restore purchase" control on the Reading tab instead of the waiting state. It runs the existing POST path, which now returns the matched session id, and saves it; the Reading then generates as normal.

**Founder access.** A `READING_ADMIN_TOKEN` env is accepted by the route in place of a session id. In admin mode (the existing five-tap gesture) the client prompts for it once and keeps it in sessionStorage. This is how the twenty voice-review Readings and the quiz validation happen without buying.

### Generation contract

- **Model:** `claude-sonnet-5` for both calls. Fast enough for the five-second teaser target and cheap enough that the bounded key space costs a few dollars to fill. If the twenty-Reading voice review finds it flat, the full Reading moves to `claude-opus-5`; the teaser stays on Sonnet.
- **Cache key:** `v1|<lifePath>|<compoundLabelKey>|<TYPE>`, prompt version first, so a prompt edit never serves a stale Reading. `compoundLabelKey` is the compound number only when `COMPOUND_MEANINGS` has a label for it (10 to 33 today); otherwise `0`, and the prompt omits the compound clause. `calculateLifePath` produces compounds from 3 to 66, most unlabeled; without this rule identical prompts would generate distinct Readings per unlabeled compound. The key space is bounded (12 life paths × about three labeled compounds each × 16 types), so generation cost is bounded regardless of traffic, and the cache holds nothing personal.
- **Prompt inputs are a pure function of the key.** The life-path entry, the compound label when present, the type entry, and the dominant temperament from `temperamentForMbti(type)`. The Reading tab's temperament pill uses the same function, so pill and text always agree. Known divergence: the Profile tab ranks temperaments by share (`getTemperaments`), and for a borderline quiz result (say N 55, F 55, P 80) the shares can rank Responder above Empath while the letters say Empath. The Reading follows the letters; the Profile is unchanged in v1; whether `dominantTemperament` should prefer the letters is a v2 question. Not the temperament shares: those derive from the dimension percentages, which differ between a 3-to-0 quiz and a typed 70/30 for the same letters, and the cache would return whichever generated first.
- **Teaser:** a separate call, about 120 tokens, same key, cached, no session required. It is the one personal result the paywall fires after, and it returns in a few seconds. Abuse control: a `READING_DISABLED` env kill switch and a daily generation counter with a ceiling checked before each call. No IP rate limit in v1: the key space is bounded and the teaser is cached, so the threat costs cents and the ceiling already bounds it.
- **Full Reading:** one call at purchase (or on first visit to the tab by a verified buyer). The server prepends the cached teaser as the Opening and asks the model for five sections, so the model is never asked to reproduce the teaser: The Number / The Type / Where they agree / Where they pull / A closing line. Total 700 to 900 words including the Opening.
- **Voice:** third person, the almanac register. Exemplars passed verbatim: the four `SYNTHESIS_BY_TEMPERAMENT` templates, the convergence themes, and the growth edges in `src/data/personality.ts`. Those blocks are already third person; the second-person copy elsewhere in the app is not used as an exemplar. Banned words and patterns: journey, energy, universe, vibration, manifest, destiny, "you will", "expect", "this year". The disclaimer framing is in the system prompt.
- **Validation and fallback:** output is markdown with exactly the five requested headings; the four body sections 150 to 200 words each, the closing line 15 to 40 words (with the teaser, a passing document lands in the 700 to 900 range without a separate total check); no banned patterns. Any failure regenerates the whole five-section document once (one prompt, one contract; no per-section prompt). If it still fails, each failing section falls back to existing copy: The Number → `LIFE_PATH_MEANINGS[n].description`; The Type → `MBTI_DATA[type].desc`; Where they agree → `getSynthesis`; Where they pull → the temperament's growth edges; closing line → a fixed line kept in the data layer: "That is the reading. Take what is useful and leave the rest, and come back to it in a year to see which parts have moved." Fallback sections are exempt from the length check. A Reading with any fallback section is shown with a quiet "regenerate" control and is not cached, so the next visit tries again.
- **Cold start:** `maxDuration` set in `vercel.json` to 60 seconds; the Reading tab shows a waiting state with the teaser already visible. Streaming the first attempt is an engineering option, not a requirement.

### Analytics

Five kit events, named in `analytics.ts` helpers: `lifepath_shown` (once per session, when a non-sample birthdate yields a nonzero life path), `letters_entered` (with `method: quiz | typed`), `reading_teaser_shown`, `gate_shown` (fired by `ProGate`, not an `analytics.ts` helper; the Reading gate reports `gate: reading`), `pro_purchase` (exists). The letters step is the one the second opinion predicted would leak; its number is recorded before any optimization. Phase 1 ships the first two and the existing two; the teaser event ships with Phase 2.

## Open Questions

- Store: Upstash Redis via the Vercel Marketplace, Vercel Blob, or a single Postgres table. Engineering review decides; the design requires the three slots named under Constraints (Readings by key, hashed session → hashed key set, daily counter), survival across cold starts, and no personal data. Vercel KV is no longer a first-party product.
- Whether the twelve quiz questions get a light validation pass (do they reproduce the founder's known type from the founder's honest answers) before launch. Recommended, one evening.
- The exact shape of the Profile tab's "no facet scores yet" state for a quiz-only reader (which sections hide, which show a prompt to paste a report). The state itself is Phase 1 scope; only its layout is open.

## Success Criteria

- A stranger who arrived with only a birthdate completes the quiz, sees a teaser paragraph, and buys a Reading. Done is the first one.
- The Phase 1 events are live in Vercel Analytics, and seven days of numbers exist, before the Reading ships.
- The teaser renders within five seconds; the full Reading within ten seconds on a warm cache and within thirty on a cold generation that passes validation first time, with a visible waiting state. A regeneration may take longer; the waiting state stays honest about it.
- Every cached Reading passed the validator with no fallback sections; the founder reads the first twenty for voice.
- Refunds under a few percent of purchases in the first month.
